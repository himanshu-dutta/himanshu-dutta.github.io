## About Me

I am currently working as a Research Fellow (equivalent to Pre-doctoral Researcher) at <strong>Microsoft Research, India</strong>. I work with the ReFoRM team under the leadership of [Dr. Manik Varma](https://manikvarma.org/). I am currently working in close collaboration with [Lokesh N](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=BkkZbo0AAAAJ&hl=en) on machine learning and information retrieval. My current work focuses on improving how dense retrieval models are trained, especially through principled negative sampling and batch construction strategies for contrastive learning. I am particularly interested in systems that learn structured knowledge from text and use it for reasoning, retrieval, and interaction.

Previously, I completed my M.Tech at IIT Bombay under [Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=vvg-pAkAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Preethi Jyothi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QN_uhu8AAAAJ&hl=en), where I worked on document-level machine translation, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic NLP systems.


## Research Interests

**NLP**, **Information Retrieval**, **Machine Learning**
